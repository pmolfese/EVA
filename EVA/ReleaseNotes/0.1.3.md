version: 0.1.3
date: 2026-07-13
title: Interpolation responsiveness and stability
---

This quick follow-up release focuses on eliminating hangs and making bad-channel
interpolation safer during interactive review and epoch processing.

- Channel interpolation is resolved away from waveform rendering and cached until
  either the signal or interpolation recipe changes.
- The existing waveform remains visible while a new interpolated signal is being
  prepared.
- Multiple interpolation changes are applied atomically, avoiding partially
  updated displays.
- Global bad channels discovered during PSA processing are interpolated together
  in a background operation.
- Interpolation work is cancelled or discarded when the recording changes,
  preventing results from an old recording from being applied to a new one.
- Improved processing progress coordination and queue behavior around long-running
  operations.
- Update checks now bypass stale HTTP caches.
