# Open And Inspect A Recording

This tutorial introduces the basic review loop: open a file, orient to the waveform, inspect events, and make a first-pass quality note.

## Goal

By the end, you should know whether the recording loaded correctly and whether obvious data-quality issues need attention.

## Steps

1. Open EVA.
2. Open or drag in a supported recording.
3. Wait for the waveform view to finish loading.
4. Check that the expected channels are present.
5. Check that event markers appear when the recording includes events.
6. Zoom or scroll through several parts of the recording.
7. Note flat, saturated, noisy, or missing channels.
8. Open a topographic view for a suspicious sample if sensor geometry is available.

## What To Look For

- Does the channel count match the recording system?
- Do event timings look plausible?
- Are there long stretches of flat signal?
- Are artifacts global, channel-local, or spatially patterned?
- Does the sensor layout look correct?

## Outcome

You should have a short quality note such as:

> Recording opens successfully. Events are visible. Several frontal channels show blink activity. Channel E57 appears flat and should be reviewed for marking/interpolation.
