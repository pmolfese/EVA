# Review Health Scores

This tutorial shows how to use channel and segment health scores as review aids.

## Goal

Use EVA's explainable metrics to prioritize manual review.

## Steps

1. Open a recording.
2. Open channel health details.
3. Sort or scan for channels rated watch or poor.
4. Inspect the metric breakdown for each questionable channel.
5. Compare the score with the waveform.
6. Open segment health details.
7. Review poor segments and check whether the score reflects artifacts, drift, or other issues.
8. Export health metrics if the recording is part of a training-data or audit workflow.

## Review Principles

- A score is a prompt for review, not a final decision.
- Metric explanations matter more than the label alone.
- Human-reviewed labels are valuable if EVA will later train models from exported metrics.
